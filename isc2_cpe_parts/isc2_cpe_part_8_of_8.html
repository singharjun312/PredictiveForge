Title

AI Security & Governance — Part 8 of 8: ISO/IEC 42001:2023 AI Management System Compliance Validator & Supply Chain Security Scanner with SBOM Generation

Synopsis

Implemented two enterprise-grade security and governance modules: (1) an automated ISO/IEC 42001:2023 AI Management System compliance validator covering all operational clauses (4–10) with 15 checks, weighted risk scoring, and downloadable HTML compliance reports; and (2) a Supply Chain Security Scanner that checks all installed Python packages against a curated CVE database and generates a Software Bill of Materials (SBOM) in SPDX-inspired format. Both modules are integrated into the PredictiveForge AI platform as auditable, downloadable compliance artifacts.

Summary of Work Experience

Designed and implemented iso42001_validator.py, a full-coverage compliance engine for ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — the international AI Management System standard. The validator performs 15 automated checks spanning: Clause 4 (Context — purpose documentation, stakeholder identification), Clause 6 (Planning — risk & opportunity assessment, measurable AI objectives), Clause 7 (Support — resource identification, documentation quality), Clause 8 (Operation — data governance, AI impact assessment, data quality controls, model validation, production monitoring), Clause 9 (Performance Evaluation — metrics tracking, audit trails), and Clause 10 (Improvement — error handling, continual improvement processes). Implemented severity-weighted scoring (Critical 4x, High 3x, Medium 2x, Low 1x) producing clause-level and overall compliance scores with risk ratings (Low/Medium/High/Critical). Output is a downloadable, print-ready HTML compliance report suitable for internal audits, client deliverables, and regulatory submissions.

Designed and implemented supply_chain_scanner.py, a Software Composition Analysis (SCA) engine that enumerates all installed Python packages via pkg_resources, evaluates version ranges against a curated CVE database (covering NumPy, Pillow, Requests, Cryptography, urllib3, SetupTools, Certifi, Tornado, scikit-learn, TensorFlow, PyTorch, PyYAML, Paramiko, Werkzeug, Flask), and generates a Software Bill of Materials (SBOM) in SPDX 2.3-inspired format. The scanner produces severity-classified findings (Critical/High/Medium) with NVD CVE links, remediation guidance (minimum safe versions), and a full package inventory with license metadata. Output is a downloadable HTML report suitable for DevSecOps pipeline integration and third-party security assessments.

Both modules are integrated into the PredictiveForge "AI Compliance & Security Validator" as dedicated tabs alongside the existing NIST AI RMF and EU AI Act validators, providing a unified governance dashboard. Work is directly applicable to CISSP Domain 3 (Security Architecture), Domain 7 (Security Operations), and Domain 8 (Software Development Security).
